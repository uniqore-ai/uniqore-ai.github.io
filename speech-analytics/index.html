Every day

For every rep: who needs a follow-up, where to set the next step, how to handle the objection, and which deal cannot sit untouched.

Example: “Sam: reopen deal 1288, quantify the ROI at the customer’s volume, and offer two specific follow-up times.”

Not “get better at objections,” but one specific skill, a proven peer example, and the deals to use when checking progress.

Any time period

Frequently asked questions.

What if we have very few call recordings?
Uniqore tells you before the run and works with what is available: HubSpot emails, notes, messages, and approved recordings. Short unanswered calls are excluded automatically, and the final review shows exactly what was included.
How is this different from call intelligence in a phone system?
Phone systems usually focus on calls. Uniqore reviews the deal as a whole — approved recordings, emails, messages, and notes — compares closed-won and closed-lost patterns, and turns the evidence into coaching and deal actions instead of a transcript dump.
Do sales reps need to install anything?
No. Uniqore runs as a desktop app for the owner or sales leader. Reps keep working in HubSpot; you share the review in a sales meeting or send the action plan directly.
