Opening Uniqore on macOS for the first time.

Uniqore is not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS may warn you about an unidentified developer the first time you open it. Here is what to do:

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Uniqore.

After that, updates are simple: Uniqore downloads them for you and installs them when you approve. No manual reinstall required.
