What you get after a review.

Not a pile of transcripts — conclusions you can bring to tomorrow's team meeting.

🏆

A scorecard with a methodology

Every conversation is scored on 8 criteria — from need discovery to first-response speed. The score is computed by code, not by a neural net: two runs give the same number.

📋

A personal plan for each rep

Two or three steps per person with examples from their own deals: asked about budget — won; didn't ask — lost. Advice without a deal reference is never shown.

💬

Your best reps' lines — verbatim

How your strongest sellers answer "too expensive" and book the next step — quoted from won deals. Teams trust their own people, not playbooks.

⏳

Stuck deals — this week's money

The review covers more than closed deals. Stuck ones — sitting with no movement — get their own pass: what's blocking and what to do to push. That's money you can still recover.

Scores you can trust. And verify.

A rep ranking nobody can verify isn't a tool — it's a fight waiting to happen. So this one works differently.

🧮

Code does the math

Criteria are normalized and weighted by the program with fixed weights. The AI answers questions like "was the price named" — it never decides who's best.

🔗

Every conclusion links to deals

Under each step of the plan — the specific deals it's built on. One click opens that exact card in your CRM.

⚖️

Too little data — no verdict

Fewer than four reviewed closed deals — no score, with the reason spelled out. A "worst rep" built on three deals is slander, not assessment.

📈

Progress from review to review

Reviews accumulate: you see how the team's and each rep's scores move period over period — whether the coaching landed.

Call recordings are sensitive.
We treat them that way.

🔒

Read-only

We never ask for write permissions — the webhook stays read-only. Delete it in your CRM with one click and access is gone.

🕶

Names never reach the AI

The model sees "Rep #1", not last names. Names, phone numbers and emails in messages are replaced with tokens before sending; real names stay in the app.

✋

Two separate consents

Message texts and call audio are processed only after your explicit "yes" to each. Without audio consent, calls stay numbers: how many, when, how long.

💻

Reports live on your computer

Reviews are stored locally, in the app. Your reps install nothing — they won't even notice.

Frequently asked questions.

What if we don't have many call recordings?
The review says so honestly before you start — and works with what's there: messages, emails, notes, WhatsApp and Telegram threads from your CRM. Short rings and missed calls are filtered out automatically, and the report always includes a "What was read and what wasn't" section.
We're on HubSpot / Salesforce / Pipedrive — can we use it?
Not yet. Today UNIQORE connects to Bitrix24 and amoCRM (Kommo); support for US-market CRMs is on the roadmap. Write to us — we'll let you know the moment yours is ready.
Is there an English version?
The product currently ships in Russian — the interface and the reports. English is on the roadmap, and early-access pilots are how we prioritize it. If that's you, write to us.
How is this different from the speech analytics in my phone system?
Phone systems count keywords against a checklist. UNIQORE reviews deals end to end — calls, messages and emails together — compares won against lost, and returns a plan: who to coach on what, with examples. And it doesn't bury you in transcripts — you get conclusions.
Do my reps have to install anything?
No. UNIQORE is an app on the owner's computer. Nothing is pushed to the team; you share the review yourself — at the meeting or as a message.
