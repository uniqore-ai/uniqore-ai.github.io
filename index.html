Frequently asked questions.

How is Uniqore different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Uniqore works from the business context you choose to connect — so it can answer with your pipeline, conversations, numbers, and operating reality instead of generic best practices.

ChatGPT and Claude remain excellent tools. Uni helps you decide when to use them, prepares the task, and guides you through the work.

Generic AI“Coach reps to improve discovery and always confirm a next step.”
Uniqore after reviewing your conversations“Rep #2 confirmed a dated next step in only 2 of 8 deals. In lost deals, prospects leave without a follow-up date. Here are three deal excerpts and this rep's coaching plan for the week.”
Which automation should I start with?

You do not need to know the product name. Tell Uni what is expensive right now — stalled deals, unclear spend, uneven rep performance, or a weak margin. He will narrow the question and point you to the fastest useful result.

How quickly will I see a result?

You can get useful answers in your first conversation with Uni. A data-backed automation needs time to connect and review the relevant records, but the goal is a useful result in days — not a multi-quarter implementation.

Do I need AI expertise or prompt-engineering skills?

No. Describe the business question in plain English or choose a ready-to-run automation. Uni recommends the next step and the right tool.

Do I have to connect every system on day one?

No. Start with one business question and the minimum useful source — HubSpot for sales analysis, or bank statements for cash flow. Add more context only when it improves the answer.

Can Uniqore change anything in HubSpot?

No. The HubSpot connection is read-only for analysis. Uniqore reviews the records you authorize; it does not edit deals, properties, workflows, or settings.

Which CRM do you support?

The US pilot is built around HubSpot. If your team uses another CRM, email hi@uniqore.ai and tell us what you use — we will assess the fastest path to your data.

What if the automation I need is not available yet?

Describe the job to Uni. He may route you through an existing AI tool, and the request goes directly to our product team. We prioritize the catalog around recurring, expensive problems from real business owners.
